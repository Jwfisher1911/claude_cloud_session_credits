# Operational Battle Rhythm

## What you will be able to do
- Start each day with one revenue action before email.
- Pick exactly three Mission-Essential Tasks each day.
- Run a weekly rhythm of revenue, delivery, financial review, and rest.

## Lesson 1: The first-light task
Your first hour sets the day. Most new owners spend it on email and admin. Revenue work slips to the afternoon, then to tomorrow.

The first-light task fixes this. It is one action, done before email, which moves money toward the business.

1. Write tomorrow's first-light task tonight.
2. Keep it under 30 minutes.
3. Do it before you open email or social media.

**Example:** A mobile notary sets her first-light task as "Call two title companies from my prospect list." She finishes by 7:30 a.m. and books one signing by noon.

> **Field note:** Put the task on a sticky note on your phone. You see it before you see your inbox.

**Your task today:** Write tomorrow's first-light task on a card and place it where you start your day.
