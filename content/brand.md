# VERC Brand Guide

## Who we are
Veteran Entrepreneur Resource Collaborative (VERC) teaches practical business education, operational systems, contracting readiness, and trusted resources to veterans, military spouses, and service-disabled veteran entrepreneurs. Website: vercusa.com. VERC is a d/b/a of Casa Concepts LLC.

Founder: Jerry Fisher, Army veteran (Panama, Qatar, Afghanistan, Iraq, Djibouti, Germany, and stateside), followed by years of federal service helping veterans start businesses.

## Audience
- Veterans considering a business.
- New veteran business owners in their first two years.
- Service-disabled veteran-owned small businesses (SDVOSBs).
- Military spouses who own or plan to start a business.

Readers are disciplined and capable. Most are new to sales, marketing, and bookkeeping. Many are busy and read on a phone.

## How VERC teaches
"Training built the way we train. Short modules, clear tasks, real results."
- Every lesson ends with a task the reader completes today.
- Use military planning language the audience already knows: battle rhythm, mission-essential tasks, first light, after-action review, standing orders, billets. Explain each term once for military spouses and civilians.
- Teach the system, then show it with one worked example from a small service business (a lawn care company, a mobile notary, an HVAC contractor, a bookkeeping practice, a transportation service).
- Respect the reader. No hype, no income promises, no guilt.

## Rules VERC follows in every product
- Never promise income, approvals, contract awards, or results.
- Never invent statistics, dollar figures, deadlines, or program rules. Use facts from the approved source files. When a fact is missing, write a VERIFY marker, for example: [VERIFY: current platform minimum daily budget].
- Do not give legal, tax, or financial advice. Tell readers when to see an attorney, CPA, or the agency itself.
- VERC is not affiliated with the Department of Defense, the U.S. Small Business Administration, or the Department of Veterans Affairs. SBA makes every certification decision.
