## Program basics
- SBA took over veteran business certification from the VA effective January 1, 2023. The final rule was published November 29, 2022. (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- The program is called the Veteran Small Business Certification Program, also called VetCert. It certifies Veteran-Owned Small Businesses (VOSB) and Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Businesses (SDVOSB). (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- Applicants apply through MySBA Certifications. The link is on the SBA VetCert page at sba.gov/vetcert. (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- Help: certifications@sba.gov or 866-SBA-HELP (866-722-4357), Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern. (sba.gov/sdvosb)

## Eligibility
- The VA must identify the owner or owners as a Veteran or a Service-Disabled Veteran. (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- The firm must meet the SBA small business size standard for at least one NAICS code in its SAM.gov profile. (SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- At least 51 percent of the business must be owned and controlled by one or more veterans. (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- For SDVOSB status, the owners must be veterans the VA rates as service-disabled. (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- A permanently and totally disabled veteran who cannot manage daily operations can still qualify if a spouse or appointed permanent caregiver helps manage the business. (sba.gov/sdvosb, SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- A surviving spouse who takes ownership and control after the veteran's death can continue to qualify. (SBA VetCert fact sheet)

## Why it matters
- The federal goal is to award at least 5 percent of federal contracting dollars to SDVOSBs each year. The FY2024 NDAA raised this goal. (sba.gov/sdvosb, SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- Under the FY2024 NDAA, SDVOSB goaling and subcontracting credit requires VetCert certification. (sba.gov/sdvosb)
- VA sole-source and set-aside awards require SBA certification. Self-certification does not qualify. (sba.gov/sdvosb)

## Before applying
- Register in SAM.gov and have the Unique Entity Identifier (UEI) and the company bank account number ready. (SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- The owner's full name (including any suffix), date of birth, and residential address in the SBA profile must match VA records. Check VA records at va.gov under the profile, and make sure both mailing and home addresses are filled in. (SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- Do not resubmit an application until SBA and VA records match. (SBA VetCert fact sheet)

## Documents by business type (SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- Sole proprietorship, one of: IRS SS-4 EIN assignment letter with the correct name and EIN, a fictitious name or trade name certificate, or the most recent tax schedule.
- LLC: Articles of Organization and all amendments as filed with the state, state certificate of formation if available, operating agreement and all amendments, signed and dated minutes showing current operating practices.
- General partnership: partnership agreement and all amendments, plus one of: most recent Schedule K-1s, SS-4 EIN letter, or fictitious name certificate.
- LP or LLP: partnership agreement and all amendments, state certificate of formation if available, signed and dated minutes showing current operating practices.
- Corporation: Articles of Incorporation and all amendments as filed with the state, signed and dated bylaws and all amendments, state certificate of formation if available, shareholders agreement and all amendments, signed and dated minutes showing current operating practices, most recent stock ledger adopted through minutes or resolution.

## Special situations that need extra documents (SBA VetCert fact sheet)
- The veteran is not the highest-compensated person: a written explanation of who is and why.
- Franchise: the franchise agreement.
- Past-due taxes, liens, federally backed loans, or unfiled returns: a letter explaining the delinquency, the amounts, and the creditors.
- The owner or a household member is a federal employee at GS-13 or equivalent or higher: a letter of non-objection from the employee's agency ethics official.
- A permanent caregiver helps manage the business: the formal appointment and the VA determination of permanent and total service-connected disability.
- Trust ownership: a trust agreement showing the trust is revocable and the veterans are the grantors, trustees, and current beneficiaries. Other trust types do not qualify.
- A consultant or contractor prepares the application: the contract between the preparer and the business owner.
- A surviving spouse who has not remarried: proof of marriage, such as a marriage certificate.

## After approval
- Certification lasts three years from the approval date. (SBA VetCert FAQ)
- The business can apply for recertification starting 120 days before expiration. There is no limit on the number of recertifications. (SBA VetCert FAQ)
- VetCert emails the account holder when the recertification window opens, sends reminders every 30 days, and sends a final reminder 7 days before expiration. (SBA VetCert FAQ)
- The expiration date appears on the VetCert dashboard under "Certifications and Designations" and in the Veteran Small Business Search. (SBA VetCert FAQ)
- The business must notify SBA of material changes, such as a change in ownership, and must voluntarily decertify if it is no longer eligible. SBA can conduct a program examination to verify continued eligibility. (SBA VetCert FAQ)
